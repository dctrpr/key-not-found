## What this settles

The earlier stencil run discarded uncertain word selections. This test allowed every combination of the 97 variable memberships as classified by the [joint color audit](../g02_joint_color_audit/RESULT.md). Therefore the negative shape results also hold for any subset imposed by correlations among those same choices. A positive shape can come from choices that cannot coexist under one physical alignment. The result gives no evidence of an authenticated key, message or prize. No checksum, derivation, history or wallet work ran.

The [opposite-color reading review](../g02_opposite_reading/RESULT.md) separately looked for a coherent second message in its eight complete modeled selections. The next source test is to establish what page displacement the retained scans can support, then check the one-sided syntax matches against that constraint.
