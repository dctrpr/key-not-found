## What was actually tested

The fixed rule keeps ASCII-alphanumeric-containing whitespace chunks, strips only 18 registered outer wrappers, preserves internal punctuation and physical hyphens, and performs no joining, case conversion or wordlist filtering. It reverses the whole token sequence before each reverse-direction phase, with no page/row reset. Twelve phases × two directions × words/initials/finals give 72 correlated readings within **one exploratory experiment**.

The printed `12-step` phrase and nearby routine/recursion prose motivated this interval. They do not establish an extraction instruction. Literal “finals” preserve a printed wrap hyphen, slash or censor star where present; they are not guessed linguistic last letters. See the [fixed protocol](PROTOCOL.md) and [source review](../issue27_body_source/SOURCE.md).

A subsequent [AI-assisted language review](LANGUAGE_REVIEW.md) read the 72 saved outputs without new transforms and recognized no unmistakable actionable instruction. This was exploratory interpretation, not a preregistered semantic detector or proof that further plaintext is absent.
