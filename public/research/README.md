- The historical 17/17 page 76 graph used a faulty greedy phrase matcher. The claim is withdrawn as evidence of an intentional selector; later independently measured boxes are a separate matter.

- The visually checked body transcript has 32, 31, 32, 22 and 25 lines on pages 75–79, totaling 142. The old transcript split `Really? Yes.` incorrectly.
- Direct ASCII, first-word SHA256 controls, physical-highlight readings and four mirror forms of a disclosed 24-number / 256-bit rule found no activity within their specified candidate/address scopes.

Historical logs are retained as redacted records of what was attempted. Their broad phrases such as “ruled out” are not current claims that entire cipher families have been exhausted. Read the scoped result and limitations in the curated method catalog.

Variant, candidate, checksum and address counts are different quantities. Correlated variants and administrative audits are not independent theories. A chance checksum match is not a solved puzzle.
