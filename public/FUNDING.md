# KEY404 funding plan

Status: planned; no token contract, fee-receiving address, token sale, or treasury is configured on this site.

## Research funding

The proposed Pump.fun token is KEY NOT FOUND / KEY404 on Solana, using creator-fee mode. Creator fees actually received by the project are intended to fund AI-agent subscriptions/usage, reproducible research runs, data/source work, and the infrastructure needed to maintain the investigation.

This means the project's creator-fee portion, not all trading fees or all token purchase proceeds. Pump.fun controls its own protocol/platform fee schedule; other fees may apply. Fees depend on activity and are not guaranteed. The current platform fee documentation is https://pump.fun/docs/fees.

Before activation, publish the real contract, official token URL, fee-receiving address, allocation details, and opening ledger. Until those exist, the site must say **not launched**. No displayed ticker or generated image is proof that a contract is official.

## Public expense ledger

Record received fees, research payments and remaining funds with public transaction references where applicable. For each funded run, publish the method, source commit, model/tool, cost/currency, sanitized receipt reference, result and next decision. An empty result can be useful, but spending alone is not progress.

Invoices must be redacted for personal identity, billing address, account identifiers, and credentials. Do not imply that a receipt or balance exists before it is published. The launch snapshot has no project funding receipts.

## Conditional BTC buybacks

If this project actually recovers the BTC described in Max Keiser's claim, the stated project intention is to use **100% of the recovered BTC for KEY404 buybacks**, and publish the conversion/trade/fee records. Execution details, timing, transaction costs and applicable constraints would have to be disclosed before any trade.

No key has been found. The existence of an authenticated 20-BTC prize address has not been established. Someone outside the project could solve the public puzzle first or keep any coins they recover. Neither recovery nor buybacks are guaranteed.

This plan is a statement of intent, not an automatically enforced contract. A token does not provide equity, a right to the claimed BTC, a right to reimbursement, guaranteed returns, or control of the investigation. Token value can fall to zero, regardless of research results.

## What is already public

The research corpus and code are available to everyone, including people who hold no token. Nostr discussions are public. Funding does not buy privileged access to clues, a private answer, or a guaranteed reward.
