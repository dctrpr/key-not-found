SCOPE NOTES

- The 20 BTC is Max Keiser's public claim. It is not independently verified,
  and no authenticated prize address is known.
- The film says that this project has not found the key. It says nothing
  about anyone else.
- "Tested, no result" covers only the recorded candidates of that method.
  It does not rule out the wider idea.
- The fifteen squares count recorded methods. They are not a measure of
  progress.
- $KEY404 is live. The film does not show its contract: the official
  contract is the one listed at keynotfound.com.
- The fee rule covers only creator fees this project actually receives. The
  film does not say that any fees have been received or that any run has
  been funded.
- The buyback commitment applies only if this project actually recovers prize
  BTC. The prize and its recovery are unverified. Nothing in the film
  promises a recovery or a return.
- The film shows no private key, seed phrase or wallet credential. The
  characters and numbers in it are the printed text of the article.
